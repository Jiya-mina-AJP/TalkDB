Here are some sample questions you can use to test the Text-to-SQL web application with the generated "student.db" database:

--- Simple Queries ---
1. List all students who have a GPA higher than 3.5.
2. Show all courses offered along with their credits.
3. List the students in class 12th who are in Section A.
4. Who is teaching the course CS101?

--- Aggregations and Grouping ---
5. What is the average GPA of all students?
6. Count how many students are enrolled in the course MATH201.
7. Show the count of students in each class.
8. Find the maximum GPA among all students.

--- Join Queries (Multi-table) ---
9. Show the names of students enrolled in "Introduction to Computer Science".
10. List all courses that Priya Patel has enrolled in.
11. List the student names, their course IDs, and their grades for all enrollments.
12. Show student names and instructors for students who got an 'A' grade.
